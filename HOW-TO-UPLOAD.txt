CARE website 3.23.0 — upload ONCE by hand (about 5 minutes)
==========================================================
What it adds: the new 💬 assistant "Care Saathi" (instant answers from your website, Hindi / Urdu, voice,
booking, works on phones in light and dark mode), the "Patient App" page + menu item + home-page section with the
Android download, and a fix: footer links on phones each on their own line.
Nothing else on the website is changed (pages keep their exact text and line endings).

1. Open https://github.com/drkhanabrar/carehospital-website and sign in.
2. Click "Add file" -> "Upload files".
3. Open this zip's folder "carehospital-website-3.23.0" and drag EVERYTHING inside it (the folders js, css, assets,
   downloads and the files app.html, index.html, doctor.html, product.html, products.html, sitemap.xml, README.md)
   onto the GitHub page. GitHub keeps the folders.
   (Not needed: changes.patch and this HOW-TO file — leave them out.)
4. At the bottom write "Website 3.23.0" and press "Commit changes".
5. Wait 2 minutes. Open www.carehospital.in on the phone and the PC (refresh: Ctrl+F5) and check:
   - 💬 bottom-left → it says you can talk in your own language → ask "Dr Abrar education" (instant answer);
   - menu → Patient App → "Download for Android";
   - footer → links one under the other on the phone.
6. CARE server (after installing CARE 3.23.0): Settings → 📱 Patient app → 🌐 Website assistant ON, Websites allowed:
   https://www.carehospital.in, https://carehospital.in ; then Settings → 🌐 Website & Facebook.
   From then on CARE can update the assistant and the Care-Connect app on the website for you (only those files).
