How to upload (same as last time)
 1. Unzip this file. Open the folder "carehospital-website-update".
 2. Open github.com/drkhanabrar/carehospital-website
 3. Click "Add file" -> "Upload files".
 4. Drag ALL folders and files from inside "carehospital-website-update"
    (app.html, index.html, doctor.html, assets, css, js, images, downloads)
    into the upload box. GitHub keeps the folders and replaces the old files.
    (Do NOT drag this HOW-TO-UPLOAD.txt — it does no harm, but it is not needed.)
 5. Write "Website update 2" in the message box and click "Commit changes".
 6. Wait 1-2 minutes, open carehospital.in and press Ctrl+F5.

Adding real reviews
 Open assets/data/reviews.js (on GitHub: click it, then the pencil icon).
 Copy a REAL Google review into the items list, like this:
   { name:"Ramesh P.", rating:5, text:"(the patient's own words)", date:"Nov 2026",
     source:"Google", doctor:"abrar-khan" },
 Never write reviews yourself. In Google Business Profile click "Ask for reviews",
 copy that link and put it in  writeUrl: ""  (or send it to me).
