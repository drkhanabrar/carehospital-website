Upload (same as last time)
 1. Unzip. Open the folder "carehospital-website-update".
 2. github.com/drkhanabrar/carehospital-website → "Add file" → "Upload files".
 3. Drag ALL folders and files from inside the folder (app.html, assets, downloads) into the box.
 4. Message "Website update 3" → "Commit changes". Wait 1–2 minutes, open carehospital.in, press Ctrl+F5.

Later updates can come from CARE by themselves: CARE → Settings → 🌐 Website → ⬆ Publish
(the app, the approved patient reviews and the assistant files).
