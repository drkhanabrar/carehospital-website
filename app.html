Install

Install in 5 easy steps

Android phones and tablets (Android 7 or newer) — a normal phone, no rooting needed. It takes about one minute.

  1. 1Tap “Download for Android”.

  2. 2When the download is complete, tap “Open”.

  3. 3If the phone asks, open Settings and switch on “Allow from this source”, then go back.

  4. 4Tap “Install”. If Play Protect stops it, see the help just below.

  5. 5Open Care-Connect from your home screen.

The pictures are illustrations — your phone's screens may look a little different.

We add new features to Care-Connect regularly. When a new version is ready, the app shows “⬆️ Update” — tap it to get the latest app from the hospital. On iPhone (Safari way) you always have the latest version by itself.

!Phone says “Blocked by Play Protect”?

On some phones Google Play Protect stops apps that are not from the Play Store. Care-Connect comes from the hospital's own website, so it is safe to install.

  1. First try: tap “More details”, then “Install anyway”.
  2. If there is no “Install anyway”: open the Play Store → tap your photo (top right) → Play Protect → ⚙ settings → switch OFF “Scan apps with Play Protect” for a short time.
  3. Install Care-Connect again (open the downloaded file).
  4. Then go back to Play Protect and switch it ON again.
First time

Open the app the first time

  1. Choose your language at the top: English, हिन्दी, मराठी or اردو.
  2. Coming for the first time? Tap “Book an appointment” — no code is needed.
  3. Already a patient? Tap “Scan the QR code on your prescription”, or type the 8-letter patient code printed on it.
  4. If the app asks for the hospital's address, it is printed on your prescription, next to the QR code.
  5. Tap “Open”. The app remembers you — next time it opens straight away.

Don't have a patient code yet? Ask at the reception, or call / WhatsApp +91 93701 11449. The app is free. Your records are kept by the hospital — the app only shows them to you.

How to use

Everything the app does

Real screens of Care-Connect with a sample (demo) patient.

Home

Your name and patient number (UHID), a health tip for today, and quick buttons for everything below.

Live OPD token

On the day of your visit you see your token number, the number now being called and how many patients are before you — it updates by itself, so you can wait comfortably.

When your number comes near, your phone vibrates, rings and tells you in your language.

Prescriptions & lab reports

Every prescription and lab report of every visit, newest first. Use the buttons to show only prescriptions or only lab reports.

Open a prescription

Tap a visit to see the full prescription, read it in English, हिन्दी, मराठी or اردو, open the PDF, or switch on medicine reminders.

Book an appointment

Choose the doctor, the day and a free time. You get a confirmation, and you can change or cancel it from the app.

Medicine reminders

“Remind me for all these medicines” sets an alarm for every dose of your prescription — with the time of day, before or after food.

Health readings

Note your blood sugar, blood pressure, weight or insulin. Your doctor sees the readings at your next visit.

Chat with the hospital

Write to the doctor, reception, laboratory, pharmacy or nurses. Replies come back in the app.

Feedback

Rate your visit with stars and a few words, or talk to the hospital management. Every complaint gets a number you can follow.

Family & language

Add your family members with their own patient codes and switch between them with one tap. Change the language any time.

Keep the app updated

We add new features to Care-Connect regularly. When a new version is ready, the app shows “⬆️ A new version of the app is ready” — please tap it and install the update (same steps as above). Your records and settings stay.

You can also download the latest version from this page at any time. iPhone users of way A always get the newest version by themselves.
