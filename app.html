How to install

Android phone or tablet

  1. Tap “Download for Android”.
  2. Open the downloaded file Care-Connect.apk.
  3. If the phone asks, allow “Install unknown apps” for Chrome or Files.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

First time in the app

  1. If the app asks for the hospital's address, it is printed on your prescription, next to the QR code.
  2. Type your 8-letter patient code from the prescription.
  3. Confirm with the last 4 digits of your registered mobile number.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the hospital's app address in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  3. Open the new Care-Connect icon and type your patient code.

Open in Safari
